# Changelog

Newest first. One line per user-visible change, prefixed `Added` / `Changed` /
`Fixed` / `Removed`. Settings › Updates renders this file, so write for the
person using the app, not for git log.

## Unreleased

- Added: every image carries one label - AI, Non-Illust or Unidentified - as a small badge at the bottom right of its tile and on the preview in the image panel, and the gallery filters by it: a filter row above Observed tags holds the source (All / pixiv / X, moved there from the header) and the label (Any label / AI / Non-Illust / Unidentified). The label is folded from what the classifiers found, in this order: not an illustration, then AI-made, then character unidentified. The image panel's Label dropdown sets it by hand, and a hand-set label survives any re-labelling. The old Show / Who / Made rows above the grid are gone; one status line offers "Label now" while anything is still unjudged, and By tag selects by label
- Added: "View Artists Tags" next to the gallery filters unfolds a row of the character's artists with their image counts; one click narrows the grid to that artist, together with the source and label filters
- Added: every image is told apart as AI-made or hand-drawn - from the artist's own flag on pixiv, from an AI hashtag on the post (#AIイラスト, #AIart, #NovelAI and the like), or, when the post says nothing, from a small detector that runs with the other classifiers. Nothing is removed for it; the image details say how the call was made ("declared by the artist", "from its tags", "detected 97%"), and flagged and tagged images are judged even without the optional classifier. Two small database migrations run on first launch
- Changed: while choosing images to delete, a tile you keep back out moves to the end of its grid (Smart's matches, By tag's grid) so the ones still marked lead
- Changed: the Clear button in the selection bar is greyed out while there is nothing to clear
- Changed: while choosing images to delete By tag, the chips stay where they are in their row; it is the images behind the picked chips that move to the front of the grid, so the selection reads in one place without the rows reshuffling

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-26

- Added: the pipeline pauses itself when the internet goes away - a request that fails because the connection is gone (not because pixiv or X had a bad moment) puts the crawl it was running back at the front of the queue, holds the whole queue, and the Pipeline page, the mini window and the crawl's toast say "paused · no internet"; the daemon keeps probing and everything resumes on its own when the connection is back. Pause queue by hand while offline keeps it paused until you resume it
- Changed: Settings › Storage is four plates - Library with a disk bar (library · other · free) and Show in folder, Database with its size and the never-in-the-library rule, Layout with each tree's count and size, Trash - and the app, daemon and schema versions in the header
- Added: Settings › Storage › Backup - Export writes the whole archive (characters, settings, the database, the images and thumbnails) as one file, a `.curio` bundle or a `.zip`, verified after writing; Import inspects a bundle first, shows every character with a merge / keep / take choice where yours collides, then merges into or restores over the archive; a damaged file is named by entry and what is intact can still be imported, with hurt images re-downloaded on the next crawl. `coreflame bundle export|inspect|verify|import` from the terminal
- Added: a network card in the rail, above Shutdown - signal bars, the download speed and latency, measured every 30 s along the same path the crawler uses; its ⓘ opens a detail view with the last 30 s of download and upload as a chart, the three figures, the edge server and your Wi‑Fi or Ethernet, SSID and ISP, and a Retest button
- Changed: Suggested tags in the character form is the Names list alone - the ranked pixiv co-occurrence rows are gone; "+ pixiv" and "+ X" add a name to that source, and the refresh button also shows the next batch of names
- Fixed: Settings › Language listed the languages with no space between the rows, and Settings › Duplicates ran the crawl-time cleanup toggles together
- Changed: the mini window, unfolded, shows one line per character run; the "X · followed timelines" sweep behind a crawl sits under that character, and a click on the line unfolds its parts
- Changed: Pause queue now also holds the crawl that is running, at its next post, instead of letting it finish; the Crawl page shows that character as "paused · resume" and a click continues it
- Added: a refresh button beside Suggested tags in the character form looks the names up again, past the day's cache
- Fixed: with a translucent theme, the "+N more" fade on the Crawl page's tag chips showed as a darker block instead of fading into the panel
- Fixed: in the character form, taking a suggested name for one source made the whole suggestion vanish - now only that source's button goes, the row stays until both pixiv and X have it, and it comes back if you remove the tag again
- Changed: the Pipeline's Completed today list is one row per character run - the pixiv and X halves are folded into one line with their counts added up, and the "X · followed timelines" sweep no longer makes a row of its own; click a row to unfold its parts. Pause queue is greyed out while nothing is running or waiting
- Changed: while choosing images to delete By tag, the chips already picked move to the front of their row - characters, tags and artists alike - so the selection reads in one place
- Changed: while choosing images to delete, the ones not marked step back (dimmed, full again under the pointer) so the marked ones read at a glance
- Fixed: launching Curio within a minute of a crash failed outright, because the previous daemon was still winding down; it is now stopped and the launch goes on
- Fixed: the pipeline mini window stayed on Curio's own desktop - it now follows you to every Space or virtual desktop, including over full-screen apps on macOS (it becomes a non-activating panel before it is first shown, the one kind of window macOS lets onto another app's full-screen Space)
- Fixed: pausing the queue and shutting Curio down lost the pause - on relaunch the queue ran, the crawl that had been running showed as "failed" on the Crawl page, and there was nothing left to resume. A paused queue now comes back paused with that crawl at its front; the Crawl row says "interrupted", "cancelled" or "failed" by what actually happened; and a click on that chip queues the job again - it skips the posts it already indexed, so it resumes rather than repeats
- Fixed: leaving Curio behind other windows for a while could silently stop its crawler - the app kept looking alive but every save and run failed with no message; the heartbeat now comes from the app itself rather than the page, a short silence (a closed lid, a nap) is tolerated, and a daemon that exits now really exits. A save the crawler refuses, or cannot reach, now says so in the form's footer instead of doing nothing
- Added: a pipeline mini window - when Curio loses focus while a worker runs, a small always-on-top panel appears after 1.5 s over whatever is in front: the focused character with its stage and progress, "N+ more" to unfold every worker and the queue (five rows, then it scrolls), new images today and requests per minute, Pause all / Resume all, ↗ to bring Curio back on the Pipeline page, a click on a row to open that worker; drag it anywhere and it slides to the nearer screen edge and remembers the spot; × hides it until the queue empties; when the last job finishes it says "done · +N new" for two seconds and closes
- Added: a tray / menu-bar icon - its tooltip follows the crawl ("Curio · Ganyu · 184/300 · +38 new"), its menu pauses or resumes the queue, brings the mini window back and quits; a click brings the window to the front
- Added: Settings › Notifications - "While crawling in background": Mini window (the default), Notifications (one system notification per finished run - "Ganyu · pixiv · +38 new" - and nothing else) or Nothing, plus Start expanded and whether the tray keeps the progress after ×; on Linux Wayland the mini window cannot float, so the option is disabled there and the setting falls back to Notifications
- Added: the image details panel is rebuilt around the whole post - a fixed preview with the pixel size and page counter, a Fullscreen button, and a Related images strip that walks every page of the post and then the images of the same artist's post on the other source that dedupe linked (click a tile or press ← →); the file row says where the blob sits in the library and Reveal opens it in Finder / Explorer (desktop app only); a copy that was recorded but not stored says so and shows the kept original
- Added: a fullscreen viewer - click the preview or press F; fit, 1:1 and 200% presets, wheel zoom around the cursor, drag to pan, Space or a double-click toggles fit and 1:1, ← → step through the related images (or through the gallery when a post has one), with a filmstrip, Open on pixiv / X and Reveal in the top bar; Esc returns to the panel on the same image
- Changed: the rail's Shutdown button asks first - a dialog says what is running (character, source, posts so far) and how many jobs wait, or that nothing runs; No has focus and Esc cancels, Yes shuts down. The old click-twice arming is gone
- Fixed: name suggestions from Wikidata could show a raw key path (“character.suggestions.lang.zh”) next to a Chinese alias; every language code is now folded to the ones the form names, and an unexpected one shows as its own code
- Changed: X images that have no `orig` size fall back to the 4096×4096 size instead of failing; each stored image records which size the source handed over and its decoded pixel size (a small database migration runs on first launch)
- Added: deleting images from a character's gallery - Delete ▾ in the header offers four ways to choose: By image (click, ⇧click a range, drag a box, A for all visible), By tag (the Show / Who / Characters chips, observed tags and a new Artists row mark everything they stand for, any or all of them, with the arithmetic shown), Smart · AI identifier (low recognition score, identified as another character, near-duplicates, unidentified - pre-checked, with a criteria panel and live counts) and All (typed name to confirm); the header turns into a selection bar with the count and a hint line, and a confirm sheet shows thumbnails, by-source / by-reason / posts-touched counts and the options before anything moves
- Added: deleted images go to a trash folder in the library first and come back with Undo from the toast; the trash is emptied after 30 days or from Settings › Storage › Empty trash; permanent deletion is a two-second hold and is refused above 500 images or 1 GB (the database gains two trash tables)
- Added: “Forget the posts” when deleting - a post whose every image went is marked visited so the next run never brings it back (on by default; All leaves it off so the next run rebuilds the character under the current rules); Undo un-forgets them
- Added: Smart's criteria can also apply at crawl time (“also apply at crawl time” in the panel, or Settings › Duplicates › Smart cleanup at crawl time): images named as another character or under the score are thrown away as they are stored; the Crawl page's Recognition card shows “rule active”
- Added: the Gallery page shows a quiet card with how many images Smart would flag today across your characters, each linking to that character with Smart preselected
- Fixed: signing in to pixiv or X from the desktop app failed every time - closing the sign-in window stopped the daemon before the session was stored
- Fixed: the desktop app skipped X on every run (“gallery-dl not found”) - it now uses the gallery-dl it ships instead of looking for one installed on the machine
- Fixed: a gallery with only a few images spread them across the row with holes between; tiles now fill the columns left to right
- Added: Settings › Characters has a default for the recognition filter (skip photos, cosplay, 3D and screenshots) that every new character's form starts with; existing characters keep their own switch
